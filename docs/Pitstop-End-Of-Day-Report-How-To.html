Pitstop end of day — how to

Follow the 7 steps on the screen. The POS does the maths. You just check numbers and type in the cash you counted.

Open it: Reports → Pitstop end of day
Move through it: tap Next at the bottom. Tap Previous to go back and fix something.
Blue box on the right: the money you should be banking. Watch it as you go.
At the end: tap Save report, then Finish day.

Before you start

  • The paper merch / raffle sheet from the outside table.
  • The cash from the inside till (the drawer at the Pitstop window).
  • The cash from the outside tin (the money tin on the merch table).
  • A note of any cash you handed out during the day (cash prizes, buying ice, etc.).

There are two lots of cash. Keep them apart.

1. Inside till

  • Cash from food and drink rung up on the Pitstop screen.
  • Card payments go through the reader marked 0070.
  • Card money is not in the drawer, so never add it to your count.

2. Outside merch tin

  • Cash from merch and raffle, written on the paper sheet.
  • Card payments go through the reader marked Flounderers02.
  • Again, card money is not in the tin. Don't count it.
How the POS works it out (you don't have to do this yourself):
What should be there = float you started with + cash sales − cash you paid out.
Money to bank = what's there − the float you're putting back in the safe.

The 7 steps

1 Today's event

  1. Check the event name. Tap it to change it.
  2. Check the date is the day the event ran. If the date is wrong, nothing else will match.
  3. Tap Next.

2 Inside till

This page fills itself in. You are only checking it.

  1. Look at Cash and Card. They should look about right for the day.
  2. Tap Refresh card sales if the card numbers look empty or stale.
  3. Check Square fees and Expected deposit — that's what Square will actually put in the bank.
  4. If Square won't load at all, see If Square won't load near the end of this sheet.

3 Outside merch tin

  1. Pick up the paper sheet.
  2. For each item, type in how many were sold for cash and the cash amount.
  3. Leave the card columns alone — those come from Square by themselves.

4 Floats and cash paid out

  1. Type in the inside till float — the cash that was in the drawer at the start.
  2. Type in the outside tin float — the cash that was in the tin at the start.
  3. For anything you paid out in cash, tap + Add, type what it was and how much.
  4. Then tap the grey button next to it until it says the right place:
    • Paid from outside tin — money came out of the merch tin (e.g. a money wheel prize).
    • Paid from inside till — money came out of the till drawer.
    • Not paid from cash — paid by card or invoice, so no cash left either spot.
This is the one people get wrong. If you hand out a cash prize and don't tag where the cash came from, the POS still expects that money to be sitting in the tin — and it will tell you you're short by exactly that amount.

5 Prizes given from stock

  1. Only fill this in if you gave away merch (a hat, a shirt) instead of cash.
  2. Type how many of each were given away.
  3. Nothing changes on the cash side. The stock comes off when you finish the day.
  4. Gave away nothing? Just tap Next.

6 Count the cash

  1. Count everything in the inside till, including the float. Type the total in.
  2. Count everything in the outside tin, including the float. Type the total in.
  3. Under each one you'll see Expected and Over / short.
  4. If over/short isn't $0, recount before you go on. If it still doesn't match, add a note saying what happened — don't change the numbers to make it balance.

7 Check it, then save

  1. Look at the blue box: Cash to bank, Square deposit, Profit.
  2. Count the cash you're bagging. It should equal Cash to bank.
  3. Tap Save report. You get a one-page PDF.
  4. Tap Finish day when asked (a manager may need to do this). That files the sales and takes the stock off.

An example, using a real day

Started with $100 in the till and $100 in the tin.

INSIDE: POS says $188 cash sold. You count $290.
  Should be $100 + $188 = $288, so you're $2 over.
  Keep the $100 float → bank $190.

OUTSIDE: paper sheet says $157 cash. You paid a $64 cash prize from the tin.
  Should be $100 + $157 − $64 = $193. You count $193 — spot on.
  Keep the $100 float → bank $93.

CASH TO BANK = $190 + $93 = $283.
Square pays in separately: card total minus Square's fees.
In that example, if the $64 prize hadn't been tagged as paid from outside tin, the screen would have said you were $64 short and the cash to bank would have been wrong.

If Square won't load

  1. On step 2, tap Show more, then tap Manual.
  2. In the Square app, find the whole day's card total for both readers added together.
  3. Type that one number in. The POS works out the merch reader's share by itself.
  4. Go back to step 3 and also type in the card quantities and amounts off the paper sheet.

Things that go wrong

What you see Usually because
The tin says you're short A cash prize wasn't tagged as paid from the outside tin.
The till says you're way over Card money got added to the cash count. Card is never in the drawer.
No sales showing at all Wrong date on step 1.
Card total looks doubled or halved In Manual mode, the combined total was entered as one reader only.
Bar takings missing Bar tabs are never in this report. Pitstop only.
Stock didn't come off The PDF was saved but Finish day was never tapped.
